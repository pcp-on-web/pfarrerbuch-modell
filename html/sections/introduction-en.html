The vocabulary for the Meta-Pastor Directory is an OWL ontology designed to provide a standardized description of church personnel data (particularly pastors) including their life stages, positions, educational backgrounds, and connections to locations, congregations, and families. It serves as a common modeling framework to link distributed clergy directories and other databases in a comprehensive meta-search and make them analyzable for historical, genealogical, and church history research. The ontology is being developed collaboratively as part of the Meta-Pfarrerbuch project (including the Association of Church Archives in the EKD and HTWK Leipzig); version 2.0.0 is available under a CC-BY-4.0 license and is continuously maintained and expanded without replacing the respective source and local systems.
